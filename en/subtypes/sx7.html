The shared core of Type 7

The following explanation is shared by all three instinctual subtypes of Type 7, drawn from our theory guide. The energy and function mappings are this site’s interpretive model.

7

Type 7: consumption that will not stop

  • Head · displacement (head → gut)
  • Passion: gluttony
  • ≈ Se (primary) / Ne

Type 7 belongs to the head center but avoids dwelling on serious questions, where deep reflection may encounter emptiness and pain. Energy sinks toward the gut, becoming a pursuit of pleasure, novelty and practical benefits. Gluttony names this endless consumption of opportunities, stimulation, plans and experiences, one after another, to avoid stopping.

Seven's perceiving function becomes clearer at subtype level. Self-preservation and social Sevens are grounded in reality: the former excels at benefits and enjoyment; the latter enjoys practical service and participation. Both strongly resemble extraverted sensation (Se). One-to-one Seven dreams of romance and unknown adventures, resembling extraverted intuition (Ne). With the same Ne, however, Eight breaks orders while Seven wanders and delights itself.

Seven is often mistakenly seen as naive or unintelligent. It can have exceptional improvisational ability and survival instinct while avoiding institutionally approved routes. Naruto Uzumaki in Naruto and Seong Gi-hun in Squid Game illustrate intelligence outside that framework. Here Seven resembles Five: both refuse to let external authority define their intelligence.

Sketch

One-to-one Seven is the least worldly Seven. Naranjo described people who seem not to belong to the ground: daily necessities do not interest them; what they crave lies elsewhere, in a better world. They are dreamers in the fullest sense.

Keyword: Suggestibility

Ichazo used suggestibility. Naranjo interpreted an impulse to color reality: when truth looks gray, imagine a brighter version and live by it. This is idealization. So5 places an ideal on an elevated totem pole; Sx7 uses an everyday version, like a lover finding everything pleasing. Optimism has the same blindness often attributed to love.

Passion × instinct

Gluttony consumes possibilities. Dreaming fixes the eyes on a sweet imagined destination while avoiding the unexciting road beneath the feet. Naranjo used Karl Abraham's optimistic character—self, others and everything are all good—to describe it. They persuade easily and are easily persuaded because they believe first. This model assigns Ne rather than Se: unlike practical Sp7 and So7, Sx7 seeks romantic images and unknown adventures. Its Ne wanders for delight rather than Eight's breaking of orders.

Everyday patterns

  • Is enthusiastic, talkative and full of contagious ideas.
  • Quickly becomes fascinated by new people, ideas and places.
  • Describes things more beautifully than they are and believes the description.
  • Has little patience with routine and details.
  • Moves rapidly to another dream after disappointment.

Commonly confused types

Romance and idealization resemble Four, which dwells on deficiency while Sx7 runs toward possibilities: one sinks and the other floats. Merging into another's world resembles Sx9, which lacks a direction, while Sx7 has too many.

Blind spots and growth

The blind spot is substituting imagination for experience: a dazzling dream avoids imperfect life now. Growth lands on the ground—finish something, tolerate ordinariness and boredom, pause after disappointment instead of changing goals. Discovering joy in real things that do not shatter at a touch gives imagination somewhere to build.
